DSA Article 40 — Data Access Framework for Vetted Researchers

Description of data requested DADSA 8(c) ▾
DADSA Art. 8(c) — The data requested must be precisely described: its format, scope, specific attributes, metadata, and documentation requirements.
  • Description of the data
  • Format
  • Scope
  • Specific attributes
  • Metadata
  • Data documentation
Data security DADSA 9(4) ▾
DADSA Art. 9(4) — Researchers must demonstrate robust data security covering confidentiality, storage periods, organisational safeguards, and secure processing environments.
  • Identified risks in confidentiality, data security and personal data protection
  • Description of technical, legal and organisational measures
  • Security measures
  • Storage period and destruction plan
  • Restriction of access and information sharing
  • Contractual clauses (NDAs, data agreements)
  • Researcher trained on data security and personal data protection
  • Whether a secure processing environment is necessary
Researcher conditions DSA 40(8) ▾
DSA Art. 40(8) — Seven eligibility conditions to qualify as a vetted researcher: institutional affiliation, independence, funding disclosure, data security, proportionality, research purpose, and open results.
  • (a) Affiliated to a research organisation
  • (b) Independent from commercial interests
  • (c) Discloses funding of research
  • (d) Fulfils data security and confidentiality requirements
  • (e) Requested data is necessary and proportionate to the research
  • (f) Research activities follow the purposes of §4
  • (g) Research results will be publicly available
GDPR Legal framework ▾
The GDPR provides the overarching legal framework for personal data protection, with specific provisions enabling and constraining research data access.
  • Art. 5(f): processed in a manner ensuring appropriate security of personal data
  • Art. 9(2): exceptions for data prohibited to process if made public by data subject and necessary for public interest
  • Art. 32: technical and organisational security measures
  • Art. 35(7): what the impact assessment should contain
  • Art. 89: derogations for research (arts. 15, 16, 18 and 21)
Research purposes DSA 40(4) ▾
DSA Art. 40(4) — Access is granted solely for research contributing to the detection, identification and understanding of systemic risks, and the assessment of risk mitigation measures.
  • Detection, identification and understanding of systemic risks in the Union
  • Assessment of the adequacy, efficiency and impacts of risk mitigation measures
Systemic risks DSA 34(1) ▾
DSA Art. 34(1) — Four categories of systemic risks that VLOPs must assess and that researchers may study under the vetted researcher procedure.
  • (a) Dissemination of illegal content
  • (b) Negative effects on the exercise of fundamental rights
  • (c) Negative effects on civic discourse, electoral processes and public security
  • (d) Gender-based violence, threats to public health, protection of minors and mental well-being
Risk mitigation DSA 35 ▾
DSA Art. 35 — Platforms must implement mitigation measures proportionate to identified systemic risks, and publish comprehensive annual reports on their risk assessments.
  • (1) Mitigation measures tailored to the specific systemic risks identified
  • (2) Publication of comprehensive annual reports
Fundamental rights CFREU ▾
Charter of Fundamental Rights of the EU — The systemic risk framework is anchored in the protection of these seven rights enumerated in DSA Art. 34(1)(b).
  • Art. 1: Human dignity
  • Art. 7: Respect for private and family life
  • Art. 8: Protection of personal data
  • Art. 11: Freedom of expression and information
  • Art. 21: Non-discrimination
  • Art. 24: Rights of the child
  • Art. 38: Consumer protection

Based on the DSA (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065), DADSA, and CFREU
