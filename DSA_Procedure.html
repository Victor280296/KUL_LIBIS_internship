DSA 40(8)

Researcher conditions
  • (a) Affiliated to a research organisation
  • (b) Independent from commercial interests
  • (c) Discloses funding of research
  • (d) Fulfils data security and confidentiality
  • (e) Requested data is necessary and proportionate
  • (f) Research activities follow the purposes of §4
  • (g) Research results will be publicly available
Legal framework

GDPR
  • Art. 5(f): appropriate security of personal data
  • Art. 9(2): exceptions for data prohibited to process if made public by the data subject and if necessary for public interest
  • Art. 32: technical and organisational security measures
  • Art. 35(7): impact assessment requirements
  • Art. 89: derogations for research (arts. 15, 16, 18 and 21)
DSA 40(4)

Research purposes
  • Detection, identification and understanding of systemic risks
  • Assessment of the adequacy, efficiency and impacts of risk mitigation measures
DSA 34(1)

Systemic risks
  • (a) Dissemination of illegal content
  • (b) Negative effects on fundamental rights
  • (c) Negative effects on civic discourse, electoral processes and public security
  • (d) Gender-based violence, threats to public health, minors, and mental well-being
DSA 35

Risk mitigation
  • (1) Mitigation measures tailored to the specific systemic risks
  • (2) Publication of comprehensive annual reports
